# Locked In GDD

## Summary
### Locked In will be a point-and-click adventure game where you are trying to find the key to the front door of the main character's house. The game will comedically offer how a puzzle is solved, in which obstacles must be overcome. These same obstacles will then be used in different challenges once overcomed, and the player will have to solve them differently.

## Rooms
### The game will take place in a small map, separated into a 3x4 grid, each space being a room, with each being an 8x8 grid. A room transition will occur when the player moves to a space in an 8x8 grid. This pushes the player one space in the direction of the new room, and the camera follows.

## Obstacles
###	The obstacles of Locked In are the main part of the game. They are in rooms with other obstacles, which form puzzles. To enter into a room that the player did not just exit, they must complete a puzzle involving the Obstacles to open a path. The objects then block other paths and form new puzzles, either in the same room or a different room, forcing the player to solve new puzzles in order to backtrack.
  
## Puppet (Hints)
### The puppet is the player's companion throughout the course of the game. The puppet gives the player hints on how to complete a puzzle, and is the annoying comic relief character of Locked In. The puppet will give different hints depending on a set of scores that increases depending on the number of puzzles solved, the individual puzzles solved, and the room that the player is in. At some parts of the game, the puppet will not be with the player, or will be too grumpy to want to give hints, and will not be available to the player.

## Movement
### Locked In is a point and click game, so the character moves to certain places when clicked. The character will move along a set path to set destinations within a certain room. When there is another object on the path, the player might need to find some way to move the object. Whether or not the object is passable will be determined by a boolean code.
